# This is a format reference, not subject material

`_example-episode/` holds one **finished, gold-standard episode** so you can see exactly what a
good `script.md` + `supplementary.md` + `quiz.json` look like — the two-voice script shape, the
spaced-repetition opener, the `### Listing N —` supplementary format, and the 10-question quiz
contract.

It may be drawn from **another subject**. That's deliberate: it demonstrates the *form and
depth* every episode must hit, regardless of subject. Don't copy its content into your subject —
copy its **structure and quality**. `INIT_SUBJECT_PROMPT.md` leaves this folder untouched.

Once you've written a strong episode in *your* subject, feel free to point new authors at that
one instead.
