# Supplementary Materials

The read-along reference for this episode (the spine of module H2). Nothing here is spoken — it
reinforces TEMPLE, the headline trap, the worked grid and the model answers.

### Listing 1 — TEMPLE: the six factors of innovation success (H3.1)
| Letter | Factor | Flow Hive example |
|---|---|---|
| **T** | Timing | Launched during the "save the bees" movement — peak demand |
| **E** | Emerging & available technologies | 3D printing (prototyping) + injection moulding (production) made the complex frame possible |
| **M** | Marketing strategies | Crowdfunding campaign, viral video, large social following → record $12M+ |
| **P** | Political factors | Government support / regulation / political climate |
| **L** | Legal & economic factors | Patent filed BEFORE crowdfunding (protects IP); cost & economic conditions |
| **E** | Era (historical & cultural) | Cultural shift to sustainability / home food production vs old "smoke the bees" tradition |

> "An innovation succeeds inside a **TEMPLE**." (Reused verbatim from the case-study material.)

### Listing 2 — THE HEADLINE TRAP: TEMPLE (H3.1) vs FFA EQ NEW LOAd (H1.1)
```text
TEMPLE  - 6 factors affecting INNOVATION SUCCESS (H3.1) — conditions around an innovation in the market.
FFA EQ NEW LOAd - 11 factors affecting DESIGN (H1.1) — qualities of the design itself.

Different lists, different outcomes, OFTEN BOTH IN ONE PAPER. Don't merge them.
```

### Listing 3 — Differentiating success vs failure (same six factors)
```text
The SAME six factors explain both:
  SUCCESS - the factors ALIGN (Flow Hive: timing + tech + marketing + legal protection).
  FAILURE - one or more go WRONG:
     Apple Newton  -> timing + immature technology (too early).
     Microsoft Zune -> timing + marketing (after the iPod locked up the market).

To analyse: identify WHICH TEMPLE factors drove the outcome (don't just say "it succeeded").
```

### Listing 4 — Worked TEMPLE grid: Flow Hive (success) vs a failure
```text
FLOW HIVE (success):
  T timing      = save-the-bees peak        E tech    = 3D printing + injection moulding
  M marketing   = crowdfunding record $12M+ P political = little govt support (worked around)
  L legal/econ  = patent before launch      E era     = sustainability / home-food culture
  -> factors ALIGNED -> runaway success.

CONTRAST (failure) — e.g. Microsoft Zune: good product, but TIMING + MARKETING went wrong
  (arrived after the iPod dominated). Same factors, different outcome.
```

### Listing 5 — Headline question: "Analyse the factors behind success/failure"
```text
WEAK answer (caps low): "It was a good idea and sold well." -> asserts, no factors.

STRONG answer (band ~6):
  - Walk the RELEVANT TEMPLE factors with real detail (timing, tech, marketing, legal...).
  - WEIGH which mattered most (Flow Hive: marketing + timing decisive).
  - Optionally CONTRAST a failure on the same factor.
  Analyse = judge which factors mattered, not a 6-item checklist.
```
