# Supplementary Materials

The revision sheet for the front half of module H1 (episodes 1–5). Nothing here is spoken.

### Listing 1 — The front-half MDP arc (early development)
```text
H1-01 Orient   - MDP = 3 phases, run as DRIPER; folio marked on PROCESS; justify-don't-narrate.
H1-02 Propose  - the project proposal: NICe (Need, Investigation areas, Criteria).
H1-03 Factors  - apply the 11 factors (FFA EQ NEW LOAd); critically analyse SUCCESS & FAILURE.
H1-04 Designers- study a designer's process; EMULATE the method (not the product).
H1-05 Research - research + experiment to develop the project; RESPOND to the findings.

= DRIPER's Define -> Research -> Ideate, run for real.
```

### Listing 2 — Front-half mnemonics + the through-line
```text
DRIPER          - the design process the MDP runs on.
NICe            - proposal: Need, areas of Investigation, Criteria for success.
FFA EQ NEW LOAd - the 11 factors affecting design (H1.1).
QISOTS          - research methods (questionnaires/interviews/surveys/observation/tests/statistics).

THE THROUGH-LINE (every folio page & Section II answer):
  DECISION -> REASON -> FACTOR -> EVIDENCE.
```

### Listing 3 — Connections across the front half
| Link | How they connect |
|---|---|
| Proposal ↔ factors | The criteria are the FACTORS made specific & measurable |
| Research ↔ justification | Research/experiments produce the EVIDENCE that justifies decisions |
| Designers ↔ method | A designer's process feeds your own method & research |
| Through-line | Every stage = JUSTIFIED decisions, not narrated steps |

### Listing 4 — The headline trap: FFA EQ NEW LOAd vs TEMPLE
```text
FFA EQ NEW LOAd - 11 factors affecting DESIGN (H1.1) - apply + analyse success/failure.
TEMPLE          - 6 factors affecting INNOVATION SUCCESS (H3.1, module H2).
Different lists, different outcomes — often BOTH in one exam paper. Keep them separate.
```

### Listing 5 — Integrated model answer (need + factors + research)
```text
Q: How did identifying the need, applying the factors, and researching ideas shape early
   development? (6 marks)

- NEED: needs analysis -> confirmed a genuine gap -> proposal (need + investigation areas +
  measurable criteria).
- FACTORS: function/ergonomics/finance translated into the measurable CRITERIA & decisions.
- RESEARCH: secondary research + experiments (test materials/joints) generated & tested ideas;
  RESPONDED to findings (e.g. chose strongest joint) to refine.
- Together: need = the target, factors = the standards, research = the evidence -> a justified launch.

Marks reward LINKING the three (not one in isolation).
```

### Listing 6 — Model answers to the other review questions
```text
Q2 (3 marks) — Justify vs narrate (example).
  Narrate = "I chose aluminium and bent a frame" (no reasoning). Justify = "aluminium chosen for its
  strength-to-weight ratio suiting a lightweight portable frame, confirmed by a load test" ->
  decision-reason-factor-evidence. Justifying reaches higher bands.

Q3 (3 marks) — Relationship between success criteria & factors.
  Criteria = the FACTORS made specific & measurable (function -> "supports 100 kg"; environmental ->
  "recyclable materials"). Factors give the categories; criteria are the testable targets the design
  is developed & evaluated against.

Q4 (3 marks) — How does studying a designer assist the MDP?
  Examine & EMULATE proven practices/processes (e.g. iterative prototyping, flexibility with
  materials) -> a disciplined, tested method + new techniques/ideas. Emulate the METHOD, not the
  product. (Method, not biography.)

Q5 (3 marks) — Why respond to research/experimentation findings in early development?
  Findings only add value when they change decisions: analyse results & modify the design (stronger
  material, adjusted dimension) -> evidence-based, problems fixed early (evaluate-refine loop); also
  produces the justification evidence for the folio. Ignored research wastes time.
```
