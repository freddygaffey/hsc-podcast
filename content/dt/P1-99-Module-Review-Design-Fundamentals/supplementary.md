# Supplementary Materials

The read-along reference for the P1 module review. Nothing here is spoken — it's the one-page
revision sheet for Design Fundamentals: the map, the two hooks, the key terms, the traps, the
forward links, and integrated model answers.

### Listing 1 — The P1 map (four topics, one system)
```text
WHAT is design?      (P1-01) Purposeful problem-solving -> product/system/environment
WHO does it?         (P1-02) Designers: nature of work, interaction & overlap, careers
HOW is it done?      (P1-03) The design process — DRIPER (cyclic)
WHAT do they weigh?  (P1-04/05) The 11 factors affecting design — FFA EQ NEW LOAd

ONE-LINE SYNTHESIS:
  "A designer solves a problem by running the design process, weighing the factors at every stage."
```

### Listing 2 — The two marquee mnemonics (must be word-perfect)
```text
DRIPER — the design process (a CYCLE, not a line):
  Define -> Research -> Ideate -> Produce -> Evaluate -> Refine (loop back)
  Evaluation is CONTINUAL; a failure loops you to an earlier stage until criteria are met.

FFA EQ NEW LOAd — the 11 factors, as a story in 5 chunks:
  DOES ITS JOB      -> Function, Needs, Appropriateness
  FITS THE USER     -> Aesthetics, Ergonomics
  AFFORDABLE & SAFE -> Finance, Work Health & Safety
  WELL MADE         -> Quality
  DON'T WRECK THE   -> Environmental consequences, Obsolescence, Life Cycle Analysis
    PLANET (its life)
```

### Listing 4 — Common traps across the module (the boundaries the exam blurs)
| Trap | Keep them straight |
|---|---|
| Function vs Appropriateness | Function = does it work; Appropriateness = is it the right/suitable solution for this user |
| Theory vs Practice | "Explain the relationship" → the marks are the two-way LOOP, not the two definitions |
| Process as a line | It's CYCLIC — continual evaluation, loop back; that's the "analyse" mark |
| Technological vs Planned obsolescence | Technological = circumstance (tech superseded); Planned = intent (designed to fail) |
| Ergonomics = "comfort" | It's the user↔product FIT, measured with anthropometric data; failure → injury |

- Designer: Marc Newson, Australian industrial designer who adapts the design process across fields.

### Listing 7 — Model answers to the other review questions
```text
Q2 (4) — Relationship of theory & practice, illustrated by a designer.
  Theory informs decisions; practice applies it; interdependent (theory disciplines practice,
  practice refines theory). Dyson: theory of cyclonic separation guided each prototype; thousands
  of failures refined the theory until it worked.

Q3 (6) — Evaluate a sleek, non-repairable, annually-released smartphone using the factors.
  Strong on aesthetics, function, quality. But non-repairable + annual cycle = PLANNED
  OBSOLESCENCE -> negative environmental consequences (e-waste, resources, poor LCA) + consumer
  cost. Balanced verdict: commercially/aesthetically successful but poor on environmental/ethical
  factors. (Evaluate = both sides + judgement.)

Q4 (4) — Why is the process iterative, and why does it matter for quality?
  Non-linear; continual evaluation; loop back on failure; repeat. Matters because each loop catches
  problems early and progressively improves the design against its criteria -> quality.

Q5 (3) — Distinguish function from appropriateness, with example.
  Function = ability to perform the task; appropriateness = suitability for the end-user/context.
  A powerful industrial drill functions but is inappropriate for young children. (Does it work vs
  is it the right solution.)

Q6 (5) — Outline the process; contrast domestic vs industrial use.
  DRIPER (cyclic). Same process in both. Domestic = individual, small scale, informal, little
  documentation (furniture at home). Industrial = teams, large budgets, formal action/time/finance
  management, specialised equipment, rigorous documented evaluation (mass production). Process is
  the same; only scale/formality/documentation differ.

Q7 (4) — How do ergonomics and WHS work together to protect the user?
  Ergonomics = fit the product to the user (anthropometric data) -> safe & comfortable, no strain/
  injury. WHS = rights/responsibilities ensuring safe making & use (WHS Act). Together: ergonomics
  builds safety INTO the product; WHS governs safe practice AROUND it -> safe by design + safe in use.

Q8 (5) — Nature of a designer's work + career opportunities.
  Nature: solve problems combining creativity + technology; adapt the design process; shaped by
  influences; collaborative; interact/overlap across professions. Careers: traditional (industrial/
  product, graphic/multimedia, fashion/textile, architecture, engineering) + emerging (UX,
  sustainability consulting, digital fabrication); lifelong learning.
```
