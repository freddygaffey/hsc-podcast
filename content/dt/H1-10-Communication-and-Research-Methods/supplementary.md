# Supplementary Materials

The read-along reference for this episode. Nothing here is spoken — it reinforces the research data
cycle, communication, the investigative report, the worked matrix and the model answers.

### Listing 1 — Research for H5.2: the data cycle (+ QISOTS)
```text
COLLECT  -> data via QISOTS (questionnaires/interviews/surveys/observation/tests/statistics; primary
            & secondary)
ANALYSE  -> find patterns / key findings
INTERPRET-> work out what it means for the design
APPLY    -> use the conclusions to inform & JUSTIFY decisions   <- the point of H5.2

Research only matters when you ACT on the conclusions.
```

### Listing 2 — Communication for H5.2 (module-3 content applied)
```text
PRESENT information & VISUALISE solutions, with methods APPROPRIATE TO THE TARGET MARKET.
  Forms (V-WGVA): Verbal, Written, Graphical, Visual, Audio
  Process (SMMR): Sender -> Message -> Medium -> Receiver
  Criteria (CAE): Clarity, Appropriateness, Ease of interpretation

Select & JUSTIFY the technique for the audience (render for a client; drawings for a maker).
```

### Listing 3 — The investigative report on an innovation (the case-study bridge)
```text
H5.2 also requires you to research a REAL INNOVATION and produce an INVESTIGATIVE REPORT answering:
  - What is the innovation? How was it developed?
  - What factors made it succeed?
  - How has it impacted society?
  - What is its future?

This is the CASE STUDY -> the on-ramp to module H2 (revisited in H2-08).
The SAME research + communication discipline serves both the MDP and the case study.
```

### Listing 4 — Worked method-to-purpose-to-audience matrix
| Purpose | Method | Audience | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Find user needs | Survey / interview | Target users | Data to analyse & apply |
| Test performance | Experiment | Yourself | Evidence for decisions |
| Gain design approval | Rendered model / walkthrough | Client (non-technical) | Clear, easy to interpret |
| Brief the maker | Orthographic drawings | Manufacturer | Precise dimensions |
| Case study | Secondary research → investigative report | Marker | Examines a real innovation |

### Listing 5 — Headline question: "Justify the research & communication methods used"
```text
WEAK answer (caps low): "I did a survey and made a poster." -> methods named, no reasons/audience.

STRONG answer (band ~6):
  - RESEARCH: match method to purpose; APPLY the findings (survey -> measurable user data -> refined
    criteria).
  - COMMUNICATION: match method to AUDIENCE + justify via CAE (render for a client; drawings for a maker).
  Match -> apply -> justify.
```

### Listing 6 — Model answers to the closing exam questions
```text
Q1 (3 marks) — Stages of using research data.
  Collect (surveys/interviews/experiments) -> analyse (patterns) -> interpret (meaning) -> APPLY
  (inform & justify decisions). The application step is the key.

Q2 (3 marks) — Selecting a communication technique for an audience.
  Match to audience/message/purpose; judge by clarity/appropriateness/ease. Client -> render/walkthrough
  (clear, easy); maker -> orthographic drawings (precise). Appropriate to the target market.

Q3 (5 marks) — Justify the research & communication methods used.
  Survey for measurable user data (analysed -> criteria); experiments for performance data (applied to
  material choice). Render for the client (clear, easy to interpret); orthographic drawings for the maker
  (precise). Each matched to purpose/audience & applied. (Listing without justification caps low.)

Q4 (3 marks) — Purpose of the investigative report on an innovation.
  Research & examine a real innovation (what it is, development, success factors, impact on society,
  future); builds research & communication skills + understanding of innovation; supports the MDP & the
  HSC innovation study. (Bridge to H2.)
```
