# Supplementary Materials

The read-along reference for this episode. Nothing here is spoken — it reinforces design teams,
roles, communication, the collaboration factors, the worked example and the model answers.

### Listing 1 — A design team: what it is and what it's used for
```text
A design team = a group working together toward a COMMON GOAL, pooling different skills.

Teams are used to:
  - generate ideas for projects
  - discuss and solve problems
  - share skills / expertise
  - gather consumer reactions
  - work on projects
  - support each other

Why: no single person has every skill a complex project needs (= the interaction & overlap of
professions from P1-02, organised).
```

### Listing 2 — Roles, tasks and responsibilities
| Element | Detail |
|---|---|
| Defined roles & tasks | Each member has a specific role/function suited to their skills (write them up — saves time) |
| Team leader / project manager | Coordinates the work and ensures communication happens |
| Accountability | Each member's contribution is equally valuable; each is responsible for the project's completion |

### Listing 3 — Communication within & between teams
```text
ESSENTIAL for successful projects. The team leader ensures communication & information-sharing
methods are AGREED UPON BY ALL members, on an agreed TIMELINE.

Methods: face-to-face meetings · teleconferencing · videoconferencing · email · online forums ·
         shared digital files / collaborative software (links to P2-06).
```

### Listing 4 — Factors of successful collaboration: the 4 C's (+ foundation)
```text
The FOUR C's:
  Common goals    - clearly defined, shared goals & objectives (long- and short-term)
  Clear roles     - each member knows their role and tasks
  Communication   - sound, AGREED communication strategies
  Commitment      - all members committed to the goals; accountability

FOUNDATION (underneath the 4 C's):
  Skilled, knowledgeable members · mutual respect · power/authority used ethically & fairly

(Answer "identify the factors" with the 4 C's + skill & respect — NOT vague "everyone works hard".)
```

### Listing 5 — Setting up & running a design team (5 steps)
```text
1. Select members for the skills the project needs.
2. Write up ROLES & FUNCTIONS for each member.
3. Design the WORKING PLAN: break into tasks, allocate time, sequence, assign who does what,
   produce a schedule everyone has. (= project management — P3-07)
4. Document COMMUNICATION methods + a timeline for when communication happens.
5. Set up EVALUATION throughout the project. (= continual evaluation — module 1)
```

### Listing 6 — Worked example: design team for an electric scooter
| Member | Role / tasks |
|---|---|
| Industrial designer | Form, styling, user experience |
| Mechanical engineer | Motor, battery, structure |
| Ergonomist | Safe, comfortable to ride (anthropometric data) |
| Marketer | Target-market research; how it's sold |
| Project manager | Coordinates members, schedule, budget, communication |

> Each has a defined role + tasks, shared accountability, and communicates via agreed meetings/files.

### Listing 7 — Headline question: "factors of successful collaboration"
```text
WEAK answer (caps low):
  "Everyone helps and works hard / gets along." -> effort words, not factors.

STRONG answer (band ~6):
  Name SPECIFIC factors and (where the verb allows) WHY each matters:
  common goals (pull one way) · clear roles (no gaps/duplication) · communication (problems surface
  early) · commitment/accountability (everyone delivers) · skilled members + mutual respect.
```

### Listing 8 — Model answers to the closing exam questions
```text
Q1 (2 marks) — Three reasons to use a design team.
  Any 3: generate ideas; solve problems; share skills; gather consumer reactions; work on a project;
  support each other. (Underlying: pools skills no one person has.)

Q2 (3 marks) — Importance of clearly defined roles.
  Match tasks to skills -> no duplication or gaps; create accountability (each knows what they deliver);
  save time (no constant renegotiation). E.g. engineer handles mechanism, marketer the market research.

Q3 (5 marks) — Identify & explain factors of successful collaboration.
  Common goals (pull one way); clear roles (no gaps/duplication); agreed communication (problems
  early); commitment & accountability (everyone delivers); skilled members + mutual respect.
  4 C's + foundation, each WITH a reason. (Vague effort words cap low.)

Q4 (3 marks) — Why is communication essential within a team?
  Members with different skills do interdependent tasks -> must share info to coordinate; communication
  surfaces problems early and keeps the project on schedule; agreed & managed by the leader. Turns
  separate specialists into one team.
```
