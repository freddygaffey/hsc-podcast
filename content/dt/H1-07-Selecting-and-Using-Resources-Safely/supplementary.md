# Supplementary Materials

The read-along reference for this episode. Nothing here is spoken — it reinforces the selection
factors, justification, safe-working principles, the worked example and the model answers.

### Listing 1 — H4.2 selection factors: SEE (on top of CPA FACE)
```text
On top of the module-2 material criteria (CPA FACE), H4.2 names THREE responsibility factors:
  S - Safety:        safe to work with, use, and dispose of (dust/fumes?)
  E - Ethical:       ethically sourced (no exploitation/conflict materials); respects IP
  E - Environmental: sustainable, recyclable, low carbon footprint (LCA)

The RESPONSIBILITY lens over every resource choice.
```

### Listing 2 — Justify selection FROM research (the H4.2 verb)
```text
H4.2 verb: "identify, select, USE and JUSTIFY... based on the RESULTS and ANALYSIS of research."

Pattern (decision -> reason -> factor -> evidence):
  "I selected ___ because its properties suit the function, it is the safe & sustainable option
  (SEE), as CONFIRMED by my testing/research (folio)."

Research/experiment evidence turns a SELECTION into a JUSTIFIED selection.
```

### Listing 3 — Using resources safely: the principles (also H6.1)
```text
- DUTY OF CARE (WHS legislation) — responsible for own & others' safety.
- RISK ASSESSMENT — identify hazards -> assess risk -> control.
- HIERARCHY OF CONTROL (ES-EAP): Eliminate -> Substitute -> Engineering -> Administrative -> PPE.
    PPE is LAST (protects only the wearer; elimination removes the hazard for everyone).
- DESIGN SAFETY IN — reduce hazards at the design/resource-selection stage, not just with gear.
Tools: MSDS (chemicals), safe operating procedures, a safety plan in the folio.
```

### Listing 4 — Worked example: selecting a finish for a children's toy (scored vs SEE)
| Factor | Justification (water-based non-toxic paint) |
|---|---|
| Safety | Non-toxic for a child to handle/mouth; low fumes to apply |
| Ethical | Supplier with fair labour practices |
| Environmental | Lower solvent emissions; easier disposal than oil-based |
| Evidence | Toy-safety standards research + a durability test (folio) |
| Safe use | Apply in good ventilation (engineering/administrative control) |

> Hits all three SEE factors + cites research + addresses safe use = a model H4.2 answer.

### Listing 5 — Headline question: "Justify the selection AND safe use of resources"
```text
WEAK answer (caps low): "I used MDF because it was cheap and available." -> one criterion, no safety/
ethics/environment, no research.

STRONG answer (band ~6):
  - SELECTION: justify against SEE (safety/ethical/environmental) + cite RESEARCH evidence.
  - SAFE USE: explain via the hierarchy of control (e.g. dust extraction = engineering; mask last).
  Justify selection, THEN justify safe use.
```

### Listing 6 — Model answers to the closing exam questions
```text
Q1 (2 marks) — Three factors when selecting resources.
  Safety, ethical issues, environmental issues (SEE). [+ material properties/cost valid.]

Q2 (5 marks) — Principles underlying safe working practices (also H6.1).
  Duty of care (WHS law); risk assessment (identify/assess/control); hierarchy of control (eliminate
  -> substitute -> engineering -> admin -> PPE last, since PPE protects only the wearer); design
  safety IN at the design stage.

Q3 (5 marks) — Justify the selection & safe use of a resource.
  Water-based non-toxic paint: SAFE (non-toxic, low fumes), ETHICAL (fair-labour supplier),
  ENVIRONMENTAL (low emissions, easy disposal); confirmed by toy-safety research + durability test;
  used safely with ventilation (hierarchy) + MSDS. Justify selection vs SEE + research, then safe use.

Q4 (3 marks) — Why justify resource selection using research?
  Makes the choice evidence-based (not assumption); objective support (test/sourcing research) the
  markers reward; reduces the risk of a poor choice failing later. Turns a preference into a
  defensible decision.
```
