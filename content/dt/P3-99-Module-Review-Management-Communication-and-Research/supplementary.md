### Listing 4 — Traps across the module
| Trap | Keep straight |
|---|---|
| Market vs design/user research | Market = will it sell & to whom (commercial); user = what does the user need |
| Primary vs secondary research | Who originally collected it — you (primary) or others (secondary) |
| Creative vs critical thinking | Generate vs judge |
| Parameter vs criterion | Inherited limit vs set success measure |
| Evaluation only at the end | It's CONTINUAL — before, mid, after |
| Collaboration = "effort" | Use structural factors (defined roles, agreed communication), not "everyone tries hard" |

### Listing 6 — Integrated model answer (research + management + evaluation)
```text
Q: How did research, management & evaluation work together to improve the outcome? (6 marks)
   — reusable drink-bottle project

- RESEARCH (primary survey + secondary data) -> identified the need -> informed the brief's
  measurable criteria.
- MANAGEMENT (Gantt + finance plan) -> sequenced tasks in the right order, within budget.
- EVALUATION (continual, vs criteria) -> mid-project leak test failed -> refine the lid; management
  absorbed the extra time; final evaluation met all criteria.
- SYSTEM: research set the targets, management kept it on track, evaluation drove refinement ->
  better, on-time, on-budget outcome.

Marks reward LINKING the three, not describing each alone.
```

### Listing 7 — Model answers to the other review questions
```text
Q2 (4) — How does a designer generate then select ideas?
  GENERATE (divergent): SCAMPER, brainstorming, mind maps -> many ideas, break fixation. SELECT
  (convergent/critical): evaluate vs the brief's criteria, e.g. a decision matrix. Generate then judge.

Q3 (4) — Why are both qualitative AND quantitative research valuable?
  Quantitative = numbers/trends (how many; objective measure). Qualitative = opinions/reasons (the
  why; reveals needs stats miss). Together: the measure + the understanding to act. Complementary.

Q4 (5) — How do management tools deliver on time & on budget?
  Action plan (all tasks); Gantt (schedule, dependencies, parallel tasks, CRITICAL PATH); finance plan
  (budget & track). MONITOR actual vs planned throughout -> re-sequence/adjust when tasks slip.

Q5 (2) — Distinguish market vs design (user) research.
  Market = will it sell & to whom (commercial). User/design = what the user needs & how they use it.
  Both research; different questions.

Q6 (4) — Select & justify a communication technique for a client.
  Match to audience/message/purpose; justify via CAE (clarity/appropriateness/ease). Non-technical
  client: orthographic = inappropriate (hard to read); choose render/3D model/walkthrough — clear,
  easy, appropriate. Contrast the less-appropriate option.

Q7 (4) — Why continual evaluation, and its link to the brief?
  Continual: catches problems early (cheap fix), drives the refine loop, keeps alignment vs end-only.
  Link: the BRIEF sets measurable criteria; EVALUATION judges against them (during & finally) =
  bookends. Criteria must be measurable.
```

## Glossary

| Term | Expansion | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **4 C's** | Common goals, Clear roles, Communication, Commitment | Factors of successful collaboration in a design team (P3.1) |
| **ATF** | Action, Time, Finance | The three project-management plans (H5.1) |
| **BMA** | Before, Mid, After | Evaluation is continual — before, during and after, not only at the end (H4.3) |
| **CAE** | Clarity, Appropriateness, Ease of interpretation | Criteria for evaluating/justifying a communication method (P5.2) |
| **CVA** | Consent, Voluntary participation, Anonymity | Ethics to observe when researching people, plus honesty and no harm (P5.3) |
| **Five Ps** | Product, Price, Promotion, Place, Packaging | What to research about competitors / the marketing environment (P4.1) |
| MDP | Major Design Project | The HSC project — a product, system or environment plus its design folio — that the course is built around |
| **NCC** | Need, Constraints/parameters, Criteria for success | The parts of a design brief (P4.1) |
| **QISOTS** | Questionnaires, Interviews, Surveys, Observation, Tests/experiments, Statistical analysis | The research methods, plus secondary information research (P5.3 / H5.2) |
| **RV GS-CDT D** | Resources, Values, Goals, Standards, Costs/benefits, Decisions, Tasks, Documentation | The factors affecting project management (P5.1) |
| **SCAMPER** | Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Reverse/Rearrange | Seven prompts to generate ideas by transforming an existing design (P3.1) |
| **SMMR** | Sender, Message, Medium, Receiver | The elements of the communication process, plus feedback (P5.2) |
| **V-WGVA** | Verbal, Written, Graphical, Visual, Audio | The forms of communication a designer can use (P5.2) |
