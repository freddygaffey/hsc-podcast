# Supplementary Materials

The read-along reference for this episode. Nothing here is spoken — it frames the MDP, the folio,
the narrate-vs-justify meta-skill, the module map and the model answers.

### Listing 1 — The MDP: three phases (mapped to DRIPER)
| Phase | What you do | DRIPER stage |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Proposal & project management | Identify/explore the need; areas of investigation; criteria for success; action/time/finance plans | Define + plan |
| 2. Development & realisation | Research; generate & develop ideas; study industrial/commercial practice; select & safely use resources; produce; communicate | Research → Ideate → Produce |
| 3. Evaluation | Evaluate against criteria (continual & final); assess impact on individual/society/environment | Evaluate → Refine |

> The MDP is **DRIPER run for real, at full scale**, on one project meeting an identified need.

### Listing 2 — The folio: documented evidence of the process
```text
The FOLIO = the documented record of your WHOLE design process (research, sketches, decisions,
tests, management plans, evaluation), presented as EVIDENCE.

KEY: the MDP is marked on the PROCESS (folio) + the project together.
  - A pretty product + thin folio  -> scores POORLY.
  - A solid product + folio showing rigorous research, JUSTIFIED decisions, continual evaluation
    -> scores WELL.
Document as you go — evidence of process beats a pretty outcome.
```

### Listing 3 — The meta-skill: NARRATE vs JUSTIFY (the Year-12 through-line)
```text
NARRATE (caps low): "I chose pine. Then I cut the legs. Then I sanded it." -> just what you did.

JUSTIFY (top band): "Pine was selected because its properties — sufficient strength and good
  workability — suited the function of a lightweight hand-assembled stool, as confirmed by the
  joint test in my folio."

THE PATTERN:  DECISION -> REASON -> FACTOR (from the 11) -> EVIDENCE (research/test).
Applies to the FOLIO and to written-exam Section II.
```

### Listing 4 — Model folio contents (mapped to DRIPER)
```text
1. Project proposal        - the need + areas of investigation + criteria for success   (Define)
2. Research                - investigation & findings                                    (Research)
3. Ideas generation        - sketches, concepts (SCAMPER etc.)                           (Ideate)
4. Development             - prototypes + JUSTIFIED decisions                            (Produce)
5. Production record       - making it, safe work, resources                            (Produce)
6. Evaluation              - judged vs criteria; impact on individual/society/environment (Evaluate)

Every section = EVIDENCE of a stage of the process, justified.
```

### Listing 6 — Model answers to the closing exam questions
```text
Q1 (3 marks) — Three phases of the MDP.
  (1) Proposal & management (need, criteria, action/time/finance plans); (2) development & realisation
  (research, ideas, production); (3) evaluation (vs criteria + impact). Map to DRIPER.

Q2 (3 marks) — Why is the folio important?
  It documents the whole PROCESS (research/decisions/tests/evaluation); the MDP is marked on process
  evidence, not just the product; must be built as you go to show a genuine, dated process.

Q3 (4 marks) — Narrate vs justify a decision (example).
  Narrate = "I chose pine and cut the legs" (no reasoning, low marks). Justify = "pine chosen for its
  strength + workability suiting a lightweight stool, confirmed by a joint test" -> decision-reason-
  factor-evidence. Justifying reaches the higher bands.

Q4 (3 marks) — Why document throughout, not at the end?
  The folio must show a genuine ongoing process; in-the-moment documentation captures justification &
  evidence, supports continual evaluation, forgets nothing; end-only = thin, inauthentic, lost marks.
```
