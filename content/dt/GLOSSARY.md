# Glossary — Acronyms & Mnemonics

Every acronym and mnemonic used across the Design & Technology supplementary material.
The mnemonics are study memory-devices; the lists they encode are drawn from the NSW
Design and Technology Stage 6 Syllabus. Acronym definitions follow the course textbook
glossary where it defines the term.

## Acronyms & abbreviations

| Term | Expansion | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| ABS | Australian Bureau of Statistics | A source of secondary (statistical) research data |
| AI | Artificial Intelligence | An emerging technology |
| AR | Augmented Reality | An emerging design field (AR/VR design) |
| ARM | Advanced RISC Machines (processor architecture) | The low-power chip family the Newton drove; now in nearly all smartphones |
| BPA | Bisphenol A | A chemical avoided in food-grade plastics (“BPA-free”) |
| BPS | Bisphenol S | A related chemical avoided in food-grade plastics (“BPS-free”) |
| CAD | Computer-Aided Design | An automated system for the design, drafting and display of graphic information |
| CAM | Computer-Aided Manufacturing | A computer program that makes manufacturing data from CAD drawings to automate manufacture by a computer-controlled machine |
| CEO | Chief Executive Officer | The head of a company |
| CNC | Computer Numerical Control | The computer control of machine tools to (repetitively) manufacture complex parts for a product |
| CSIRO | Commonwealth Scientific and Industrial Research Organisation | Australia's national science agency (a research/funding agency) |
| FM | Frequency Modulation | The radio band (e.g. the Zune's FM radio) |
| FSC | Forest Stewardship Council | Certification of responsibly sourced timber (“FSC-certified”) |
| HSC | Higher School Certificate | The NSW senior-secondary credential and its external examination |
| IoT | Internet of Things | Networked smart devices (an emerging technology) |
| IP | Intellectual Property | Ownership of a product of the intellect with commercial value — copyright, patents, business methods and industrial processes |
| LCA | Life Cycle Analysis | Evaluation of the resources and impacts across a product's whole life (cradle-to-grave); one of the factors affecting design |
| LEED | Leadership in Energy and Environmental Design | A green-building rating (Central Park earned LEED Gold) |
| MDF | Medium-Density Fibreboard | A manufactured timber board (an example material) |
| MDP | Major Design Project | The HSC project — a product, system or environment plus its design folio — that the course is built around |
| MSDS | Material Safety Data Sheet | Describes the identity, hazard information, precautions for use and safe handling of a hazardous substance |
| NGO | Non-Governmental Organisation | A non-profit body that can set standards / build confidence |
| NSW | New South Wales | The Australian state (e.g. the NSW WHS Act 2011) |
| OHS | Occupational Health and Safety | Workplace safety; the former framework/Act replaced by WHS (also written OH&S) |
| PDA | Personal Digital Assistant | The handheld category Apple coined for the Newton |
| PPE | Personal Protective Equipment | A device or appliance designed to be worn individually to protect the user against potential hazards (the last resort in the hierarchy of control) |
| QC | Quality Control | Checking output against quality standards (e.g. line sampling) |
| R&D | Research and Development | The investigative/experimental work of developing a product |
| SOP | Safe Operating Procedure | Step-by-step safe-use instructions for a tool or process |
| SWP | Safe Work Procedure | Step-by-step safe-work instructions (used like an SOP) |
| UAV | Unmanned Aerial Vehicle | A drone |
| UI | User Interface | The on-screen controls/layout a user interacts with |
| UX | User Experience | The overall experience of using a product (UX design) |
| VR | Virtual Reality | An emerging design field (AR/VR design) |
| WHS | Work Health and Safety | Rights & responsibilities for a safe workplace; the WHS Act 2011 sets the framework of duties workplaces must comply with |
