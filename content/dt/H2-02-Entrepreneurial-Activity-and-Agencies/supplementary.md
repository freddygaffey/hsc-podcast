# Supplementary Materials

The read-along reference for this episode. Nothing here is spoken — it reinforces entrepreneurship,
the agencies, IP/patents, the worked path and the model answers.

### Listing 1 — Entrepreneurial activity
```text
NATURE: taking an idea/innovation and turning it into a viable, usually profitable, venture —
        while BEARING THE RISK (time + money, no guarantee).
ROLE:   find new uses for ideas and bring innovations TO MARKET (idea -> real product).
MANAGEMENT: running the venture (action/time/finance — the H1 management skills).
LEGAL & ETHICAL: protect IP, honesty with investors, responsible risk.
```

### Listing 2 — Agencies that influence innovation (GASP-I)
| Letter | Agency | How it helps/hinders |
|---|---|---|
| **G** | Government | Funding, policy, support (sustainable-living support; CSIRO research) |
| **A** | Associations / standards | Standards Australia, NGOs — set rules, build confidence |
| **S** | Sponsors / funders | Investors providing capital; crowdfunding platforms |
| **P** | Patent / IP offices | IP Australia / Patents Office — protect the design |
| **I** | Industry partners | Manufacturers/businesses that develop & distribute it |

> Agencies map onto TEMPLE's **political** (government/standards) and **legal-economic** (funding/
> patents) factors.

### Listing 3 — Intellectual property & patents (a key legal-ethical issue)
```text
A PATENT (via IP Australia's Patents Office) = exclusive rights to a design -> protects it from copycats.

CAUTIONARY TALE: the Australian powerboard inventor (1971) FAILED to patent it -> lost the rights/rewards.
GOOD PRACTICE:   the Flow Hive inventors PATENTED before crowdfunding -> protected at the moment it
                 went public & copyable.

Lesson: PATENT FIRST, then reveal. Failing to protect IP is a common way innovators lose out.
```

### Listing 4 — Worked example: the Flow Hive's entrepreneurial path → agencies
```text
1. Self-funded years of R&D                  -> bore the RISK (entrepreneurial).
2. Filed a patent (IP Australia) before launch -> PATENT OFFICE agency; protected IP.
3. Crowdfunding on Indiegogo (record $12M+)   -> SPONSOR/FUNDER agency; raised capital + validated demand.
4. Partnered with industry (injection moulding) -> INDUSTRY agency; manufactured at scale.

At each step an AGENCY enabled the entrepreneurial activity. (Entrepreneur + agencies = the answer.)
```

### Listing 5 — Headline question: "Discuss the influence of entrepreneurial activity"
```text
WEAK answer (caps low): "Entrepreneurs take risks and start businesses." -> definition, no influence/example.

STRONG answer (band ~6):
  - Define entrepreneurial activity (idea -> venture, bearing risk).
  - Explain its INFLUENCE (brings to market, attracts investment, manages the venture).
  - EXEMPLIFY with a real innovation + the AGENCIES involved (patent, crowdfunding, industry).
  - Weigh the legal-ethical issues (IP).
```

### Listing 6 — Model answers to the closing exam questions
```text
Q1 (2 marks) — Define entrepreneurial activity.
  Turning an idea/innovation into a viable, profitable venture while bearing the risk; bringing it to
  market, attracting investment, managing it. (Not just "starting a business".)

Q2 (3 marks) — Three agencies + how one influences success.
  Government; sponsors/funders (investors, crowdfunding); patent/IP office (+ standards, industry).
  E.g. government funding & policy (CSIRO) lowers cost/risk -> better chance of reaching market. [GASP-I]

Q3 (5 marks) — Discuss the influence of entrepreneurial activity on a successful innovation.
  Flow Hive: inventors self-funded (risk); patented via IP Australia before launch; raised $12M+ via
  Indiegogo (validated demand); partnered with industry to manufacture. Entrepreneurial drive +
  agencies turned the idea into a global product. (Bare definition caps low.)

Q4 (3 marks) — Why protect intellectual property?
  A patent gives exclusive rights -> prevents copying -> protects the investment & rewards. Flow Hive
  patented before crowdfunding; the powerboard inventor failed to patent & lost out. Patent first.
```
