# Supplementary Materials

The read-along reference for this capstone. Nothing here is spoken — it reinforces the report, the
Section III format, the essay engine, the worked answer and the model skeletons.

### Listing 1 — The investigative report (H5.2) = your case study
```text
Research a REAL innovation (data cycle: collect -> analyse -> interpret -> apply) and answer:
  - What is the innovation? How was it developed?
  - What factors made it succeed?
  - How has it impacted society & the environment?
  - What is its future?

This report = your CASE STUDY = the deep knowledge you bring to Section III.
Recommended: the FLOW HIVE (you can grade it on every framework).
```

### Listing 2 — Section III format + the THREE marking criteria
```text
Format: ONE question, 15 marks, ~40 minutes.

Marked on (from the paper):
  1. Demonstrate KNOWLEDGE & UNDERSTANDING relevant to the QUESTION.
  2. Communicate ideas & information using RELEVANT EXAMPLES.
  3. Present a LOGICAL and COHESIVE response.

=> Answer the ACTUAL question, use your case study as the example, structure it clearly.
   (A brain-dump of everything about the Flow Hive scores poorly.)
```

### Listing 3 — The essay engine (assemble the H2 frameworks)
```text
Pick the framework the QUESTION calls for, apply it to your innovation, then JUDGE:

  success?            -> TEMPLE (timing/emerging tech/marketing/political/legal-economic/era)
  emerging technology?-> LESEE (legal/ethical/social/economic/ecological)
  ethics & society?   -> SIRRI (sustainable tech/IP/designer rights/society/Australian impact)
  trends / history?   -> SPEEG (trend drivers) / C-CWT (historical-cultural)

ONE-LINER: "TEMPLE the success, LESEE the tech, SIRRI the ethics, SPEEG & C-CWT the context, then JUDGE."

STRUCTURE every answer: thesis intro -> framework-driven body paragraphs (with evidence) -> judgement.
```

### Listing 4 — Worked Flow Hive Section III answer (annotated to criteria)
```text
Q: "Analyse the factors behind a successful innovation + discuss its impact on society & environment."

INTRO (thesis): the Flow Hive succeeded via several innovation-success factors; impact largely
  positive but mixed.
BODY 1 — SUCCESS (TEMPLE): timing (save-the-bees), emerging tech (3D printing/injection moulding),
  marketing (crowdfunding $12M+), legal (patent before launch). Marketing + timing decisive.
BODY 2 — IMPACT (SIRRI + ISE): social (engaged public, supported hobbyists); environmental (bee
  welfare, home food production) vs plastic-component footprint; ethical (IP protected).
CONCLUSION (judgement): a successful innovation whose social/environmental benefits outweigh modest costs.

Criteria hit: knowledge relevant to the question + Flow Hive as the example + logical structure.
```

### Listing 5 — Band 3 vs band 6 (same innovation, same facts)
```text
BAND 3: tells the Flow Hive story (what it is, that it did well) — no frameworks, no judgement (brain-dump).
BAND 6: shaped to the question — analyses success via TEMPLE language with specific evidence ($12M,
        patent, timing), weighs impact on society & environment, reaches a reasoned judgement.

The lift from 3 -> 6 is NOT more facts — it's STRUCTURE, FRAMEWORKS and JUDGEMENT.
```

### Listing 6 — Section III model answer skeletons
```text
Q1 (3 marks) — Three Section III marking criteria.
  Knowledge & understanding relevant to the question; relevant examples; logical & cohesive response.

Q2 (3 marks) — How to structure a 15-mark response.
  Intro with thesis (answers the question) -> 2-3 framework-driven body paragraphs (with evidence) ->
  conclusion with a judgement.

Q3 (15 marks) — Analyse success factors + discuss impact (Flow Hive).
  Intro thesis. Body 1 (TEMPLE): timing/tech/marketing/legal — marketing+timing decisive. Body 2
  (SIRRI+ISE): social/environmental/ethical impact, weighed. Conclusion: benefits outweigh costs.
  (Story-only without frameworks/judgement caps mid-band.)

Q4 (2 marks) — Why answer the specific question, not brain-dump?
  Marks require RELEVANCE to the question (criterion 1); shaping your innovation knowledge to the
  question earns relevance & cohesion, while a brain-dump wastes time on irrelevant, unfocused content.
```
