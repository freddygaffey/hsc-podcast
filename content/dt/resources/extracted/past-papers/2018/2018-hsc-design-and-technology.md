Use the multiple-choice answer sheet for Questions 1–10.

1    A graphic designer has developed a new range of birthday cards.

     Which combination of factors is most likely to determine the success of the designs?

     A.     Cost and function
     B.     Cost and aesthetics
     C.     Durability and function
     D.     Durability and aesthetics

2    Which method would be the most effective to determine the target market of a consumer
     product?

     A.     Employ an entrepreneur to evaluate the product
     B.     Gather a focus group to complete a questionnaire about the product
     C.     Post a comment about the product on social media and ask for responses
     D.     Take a prototype of the product to the local shopping centre and ask shoppers for
            their opinions

3    At which part of the design process would a designer know that a product is viable?

     A.     Production phase
     B.     Ongoing evaluation
     C.     Idea generation phase
     D.     Research of current products

                                            –2–

4   An innovative gardening tool which helps the elderly plant seeds has been designed. The
    designer has developed some initial drawings and sketches and has created a company to
    produce the tool.

    What will the designer need to do to protect the initial ideas?

    A.    Apply for a patent
    B.    Trademark the name
    C.    Register the business
    D.    Contact Standards Australia

5   Which of the following best outlines why designers would use 3D modelling when
    presenting their designs to manufacturers?

    A.    To ensure that the design is aesthetically appealing
    B.    To clarify any issues that may occur during production
    C.    To allow the manufacturers to visualise the final design
    D.    To show the manufacturers what the product must achieve

6   A design team is about to start developing a new range of products. Part of this process
    is the implementation of management plans.

    These plans ensure that the products

    A.    meet the intended purpose.
    B.    address all legal requirements.
    C.    are developed on time and on budget.
    D.    meet the needs of the target market.

7   Which of the following best outlines why designers undertake research and
    experimentation when developing a product?

    A.    To avoid making mistakes
    B.    To determine the properties of the materials to be used
    C.    To ensure all the materials are available for production
    D.    To ensure the best tools, materials and techniques are used

                                            –3–

8    A designer wants to create a new computer game and has established the design brief.

     What will be included in the next step?

     A.   Developing a prototype
     B.   Looking at similar products on the market
     C.   Completing sketches and working drawings
     D.   Identifying the limitations and doing working drawings

9    Many new homes are designed with ‘open plan’ living spaces.

     What is likely to have most influenced this design?

     A.   Social trends
     B.   Cultural diversity
     C.   Technological change
     D.   The increase in household numbers

10   Which combination of factors is the most important for a designer to consider when
     developing a new app?

     A.   Political and legal implications
     B.   Marketing strategies and timing
     C.   Economic and historical influences
     D.   Cultural impact and emerging technologies
