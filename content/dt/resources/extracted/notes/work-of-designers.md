1.1 Design is Everywhere
 Design impacts all aspects of daily life

 Shapes the way we live, work, travel, and communicate

 Examples: smartphones, transport, packaging, furniture

 Good design considers function, aesthetics, and sustainability

 Designers respond to society’s needs and wants

   1.2 Investigating Designers &
     the Nature of their Work

• Designers come from a range of fields (fashion, industrial,
  graphic, multimedia, etc.)
• They solve problems by combining creativity + technology
• Influences: culture, history, environment, personal experience
• Designers often work collaboratively with engineers, clients, and
  manufacturers
• Case studies/examples of well-known designers can inspire
  innovation

1.3 Design & Production Career Opportunities

     Wide career pathways: Industrial/Product designer, Graphic/Multimedia
     designer, Fashion/Textile designer, Architect/Interior designer,
     Engineer/Technologist

     Emerging fields: UX/UI design, sustainability consulting, digital fabrication

     Designers must balance creativity with practical production skills

     Lifelong learning: careers evolve with new technologies + global trends

    1.4 Processes Undertaken when
               Designing

•   Design process stages:
•   1. Identify a problem/need
•   2. Research and investigate
•   3. Generate ideas and sketch
    concepts
•   4. Develop + prototype
•   5. Test, evaluate, and refine
•   6. Produce and communicate the
    solution
•   Iterative process → feedback +
    continual improvement
•   Importance of risk management +
    sustainability throughout
