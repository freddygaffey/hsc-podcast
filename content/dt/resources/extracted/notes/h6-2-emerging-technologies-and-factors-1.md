 EMERGING TECHNOLGIES
• “TECHNOLIGIES” ARE THE PROCESSES THAT HUMANS USE TO INCREASE THEIR
  CONTROL OVER AND UNDERSTADNING OF THE MATERIAL ENVIRONMENT IN
  WHICH THEY OPERATE.

• TECHNOLIGIES COULD BE USED TO DESCRIBE MATERIALS,TOOLS AND
  TECHNIQUES.

• NEW AND EMERGING TECHNOLOGIES = RECENTLY / IN PROCESS OF BEING
  DEVELOPED.
