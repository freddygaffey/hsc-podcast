# M5 — Advanced Mechanics podcast plan

**Module:** M5 (Year 12, HSC) · **The first Year 12 module.** Everything here is Year 11
mechanics (kinematics, forces, energy) turned up a level and pushed into two dimensions and into
space. If Module 1–2 taught *straight-line* motion and forces, Module 5 teaches motion that
**curves**: projectiles arcing, objects going in circles, and planets and satellites falling
forever around a mass.

**The extended-response problem these episodes fix.** Students arrive able to plug into
`v = u + at` in a straight line, then freeze the moment motion goes two-dimensional. The three
classic failures: (1) not **resolving** projectile motion into two independent one-dimensional
problems; (2) treating centripetal force as a new "extra" force instead of the **net** of real
forces pointing to the centre; (3) sign errors and hand-waving on **gravitational energy** and
orbits. HSC markers want each analysis to start from the right model — *"resolve into components",
"the net force is centripetal", "U is negative and zero at infinity"* — not a plugged-in formula.

**Target listener:** a student who has finished Year 11, is comfortable with `SUVAT` in a line
and `F = ma`, and is meeting circular motion and universal gravitation for the first time.

## Marquee mnemonics coined in this module (record once, reuse identically — STYLE §5.4)
- **Projectiles — "SPLIT then SUVAT":** resolve into **horizontal** (constant velocity) and
  **vertical** (constant acceleration `g`) — two independent 1-D problems sharing one clock (time).
- **Circular motion — "NET-to-the-centre":** centripetal force is never a new force you *add*; it
  is the **name for the net of the real forces** (gravity, tension, normal, friction) resolved
  toward the centre. Find the real forces first, then set their inward sum equal to `mv²/r`.
- **Gravitational energy — "Zero at infinity, negative everywhere":** `U = −GMm/r`. Bound orbits
  have **negative total energy**; escape means getting total energy up to zero.
- **Orbits — "Higher is slower but bigger-energy":** raise the orbit and orbital speed *drops*
  (`v = √(GM/r)`) while total energy *rises* (toward zero) — the counter-intuitive exam trap.

**Sequencing logic:** projectile (2-D kinematics) → circular (2-D forces) → gravitation (the force
that *causes* the grandest circular motion) → orbital energy (the energy view) → review. Each
episode's spaced-rep opener recaps the previous one plus the relevant Year 11 lesson.
