# Physics case studies — how to handle them (plain version)

## Short version (if you read nothing else)

Keep the case studies as their **own separate, browsable collection** — rebuilt from
your doc, with images and a "which module" tag. Then in each physics episode that has a
matching case study, add a **short mention + a tap-to-jump link**.

That's it. You get *"separate interesting science"* **and** *"woven into the lessons"* —
without repeating anything or bloating the episodes.

**I recommend this. If you just say "go", that's what I'll do.**

---

## The same topic, three ways

Take one topic: **Rutherford's gold foil experiment** (how we discovered the nucleus).

### Way A — Keep them separate, link them in  ← recommended
- The **physics episode** teaches the nuclear atom. Partway through, two sentences:
  *"A few particles bounced almost straight back — Rutherford was stunned; he compared it
  to a shell bouncing off tissue paper. There's a full case study on this experiment."*
  → plus a tappable **"Case study →"** link.
- The **case study** is its own episode: the people, the drama, the apparatus, the story.
  It also sits in a **Case Studies list** you can browse anytime, like a science podcast.
- Both exist. They're linked. Nothing is told twice at full length.

### Way B — Merge them into one
- No separate case study anymore. The episode becomes **one long thing**: the physics and
  the whole Rutherford story fused together.
- The browsable "interesting science" collection **disappears**.

### Way C — Just a menu
- The physics episode **doesn't mention** the story at all. There's just a "Related case
  studies" list on the module that you'd have to go find.
- Separate, but never actually woven into the lesson.

---

## Why A is the one

- You said you want them **separate AND woven in**. A is the only option that does both.
- No episode is bloated, and no topic is taught twice.
- **Lowest risk** — I barely touch your solid physics episodes; the effort goes into making
  the **case studies** genuinely great (rebuilt from your doc, with images).

## What A costs you
- You give up the "one seamless fused episode" feel. Honestly, a short mention + a one-tap
  jump gets you ~95% of that, and the case studies are better as their own tight stories
  than as a detour inside a physics lecture.
